Your tree stays in your browser

The trees you enter or open are computed entirely in your browser. They are not sent to this website or to anyone else, and they are not stored.

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by ALL-INKL.COM – Neue Medien Münnich, Hauptstraße 68, 02742 Friedersdorf, Germany, on servers in Germany, under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). When you open a page, the web server automatically records technical data in log files: your IP address in shortened form, date and time, the page requested, the browser and operating system, and the referring page. This is necessary to deliver the website and to keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The log files are deleted after 14 days. See the privacy information of ALL-INKL.COM.

Python in your browser

To compute the trees, the page loads the Python runtime Pyodide from cdn.jsdelivr.net and the open-source package dtree-encoding from pypi.org. Your browser connects to these servers directly, so they receive your IP address and technical data of the request. This is necessary to provide the function you use (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the privacy policies of jsDelivr and PyPI.

Counting visits (GoatCounter)

To learn how often the Explorer is used, visits are counted with GoatCounter (goatcounter.com). GoatCounter sets no cookies and stores no personal data: it does not store IP addresses, and it cannot recognize you on a later visit. It records the page, the referring website, the browser, the screen size and the country, as well as a few actions on the page (for example, which example was loaded). The counting script is loaded from gc.zgo.at, which receives your IP address for the request. Legal basis is our interest in knowing how the Explorer is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GoatCounter's privacy policy.
